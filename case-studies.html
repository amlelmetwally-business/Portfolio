Case study 02 — Startup recruitment

How did social media help an unknown startup build a local name and a full team?

2M+ Organic views
50K+ Profile views
Hundreds Applicant messages

Challenges

  • An early-stage Egyptian startup with an almost unknown name
  • A small team that urgently needed to grow
  • No budget for ads or events

Goals

  • Go beyond reach and views: build recognition within the company’s own city
  • Attract local job seekers and turn their interest into actual hires

Plan

  • EGC-style content and community management, entirely without a paid budget
  • Content aimed at the youth in the company’s own city, encouraging them to apply and join

Results

  • The results shown in the images are for one platform only, over a specific period.
  • +2M organic views across platforms and +50,000 profile views
  • Hundreds of messages from interested applicants across every platform
  • A large number of hires made directly through social media, and the brand became recognized locally
