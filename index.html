About me

Writing has been part of me since childhood, long before it became a career.
At university, I discovered marketing, and found the perfect place to put that instinct to work, because "converting talent into work is real survival." I paired it with my degree in software engineering, logic with storytelling,
and more than four years ago turned it into a job, moving through content creation and social media management across a different mix of industries, building brand presence across the Egyptian and Saudi markets.

My work is built on strategy and measurable results, not just aesthetics. Every campaign has a clear goal we write toward, and creativity stays the first partner.
